Local delivery, made simple

Habichou is a food delivery application that connects you with restaurants, snacks, groceries, pharmacies and other local shops near you. Browse menus, pick what you like, place your order in a few taps and pay in cash when your delivery arrives.

From a hot meal to your weekly shopping, Habichou brings the shops of your neighbourhood to your fingertips — simple, fast and friendly.

Discover local shops

Build your cart, customise your items and confirm your delivery address — your order is on its way.

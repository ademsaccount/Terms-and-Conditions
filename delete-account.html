Delete your account

Habichou – Food Delivery Application

Effective date: November 26, 2026

How to request deletion

Deleting your Habichou account is free and takes one email. Send a request to ademseddikadem@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: "Delete my account";
  • The email address you used to register (the one linked to your account);
  • Optionally, any specific data you want removed first.

We may reply to confirm that the request really comes from you — for example by asking you to sign in or to re-send the request from your registered address. This protects you from someone else deleting your account.

What gets deleted

Once your request is confirmed, we permanently delete:

  • Your account profile: name, email address, phone number, profile photo, language setting;
  • Your saved delivery addresses and their coordinates;
  • Your carts and saved carts (saved menus);
  • Your push-notification token and in-app notifications;
  • Your order chat messages, ratings and comments;
  • Your free-form delivery requests and their photos;
  • Your profile photo from our file storage.

Order records linked to completed deliveries may be kept only as long as required for accounting or legal obligations, limited to what the law requires. No new use of your data happens after deletion, and we do not keep your account to send you marketing — Habichou runs no advertising.

Timeframe

We process deletion requests and remove your data within 30 days of receiving a valid request, and we confirm completion by email. Some copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short technical period before being overwritten automatically.

Information already shared with a shop or a delivery person for a specific completed order cannot be recalled from them.

Before you delete everything

  • You can simply stop using the app — nothing is charged, Habichou is cash on delivery only.
  • You can email us to correct your data instead of deleting it.
  • See the Privacy Policy for your full rights, or the Terms of Service for the rules of the service.

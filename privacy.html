Privacy Policy

Habichou – Food Delivery Application

Effective date: November 26, 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Habichou is a food and local delivery application operating in Tunisia. For the purposes of Tunisian data protection law, the data controller of your personal data is Adem Seddik, Tunisia.

For any question about this Privacy Policy or about your personal data, contact us at ademseddikadem@gmail.com. We answer every request as quickly as we can.

2. The data we collect

We only collect what we need to run the service. This falls into the following groups.

Account data

When you create an account, we store your email address, your full name, an optional phone number, an optional profile photo, your language preference, and — if you registered with a password — a protected hash of your password (we never store the password itself).

Google account data

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive from Google your email address, your name, your profile picture if stored, and your Google account identifier. This data is obtained only through Google Sign-In with the scopes openid, email, profile. Google accounts are not asked for a password; the email address must be verified by Google before sign-in succeeds.

Location data

When you use the map or save a delivery address, the app asks for your location only while the app is open in the foreground. Habichou does not track your location in the background. The coordinates you confirm are stored with the address you save, together with the address text and the nearest street name.

Order and address data

We store your delivery addresses (label, type, address text, coordinates), your order history (items, quantities, notes, options, prices, delivery fee, order status), your active cart and any carts you save for later, your ratings and comments on delivered orders, your messages in the order chat, and any free-form delivery request you send (description, optional photo, optional budget).

Photos

You can upload a profile photo when you register and photos with a free-form delivery request. Photos are stored in our cloud file storage and are visible only to the people who need them to handle your request.

Device and push-notification tokens

To send you order notifications, the app registers an Expo push token with your account, together with your language preference so notifications arrive in the right language. We do not collect device identifiers, phone model, contacts, or any advertising ID, and we run no analytics or tracking software in the app.

3. How we use your data

  • Account data — to create and secure your account, sign you in, remember your language, and send you account emails (email verification, password reset).
  • Google account data — used only to provide sign-in and account features: identifying your account, signing you in, and filling in your name and profile picture. Nothing else.
  • Location data — to show shops near you, to pre-fill and verify the delivery address you pick on the map, and to compute delivery estimates.
  • Order and address data — to process your order, deliver it to the right place, show you order status in real time, and keep a record of your orders.
  • Photos — to display your profile photo and to let the team understand and answer your delivery request.
  • Push tokens — to send you order notifications (confirmation, preparation, on the way, delivered) and account messages.
  • All of the above — to prevent fraud and abuse (for example repeated fake orders or attacks on the service) and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that power Habichou, each receiving only what it needs:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and file storage (profile photos, request photos). It stores the data on our behalf.
  • Render — hosts our backend API, which processes your orders and account.
  • Expo push notification service (through Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android and Apple APNs on iOS) — delivers your push notifications. It receives your push token and the notification text.
  • Mailjet — sends transactional account emails (email verification code, password reset code). It receives your email address and the code.
  • Google — verifies your Google Sign-In. We send only the Google ID token; Google receives your sign-in request under its own privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — converts map coordinates into an address when you save one. Only the coordinates being resolved are sent.
  • OSRM (public routing server) — estimates route and delivery time. Only the start and end coordinates of a route are sent.
  • Map tile providers (OpenFreeMap, Esri, AWS) — draw the map on your screen. They receive the map area your screen requests.

The delivery person and the shop see only what is needed for the order: your name, the delivery address and the order contents. Payments are made in cash on delivery — no payment provider receives your data, and no card data is ever collected by Habichou.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Habichou's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, this means:

  • Google user data (email address, name, profile picture, Google account identifier) is used only to provide the sign-in and account features of the app.
  • It is not used to serve personalised advertising, to build advertising profiles, or to train AI models.
  • It is not sold or transferred to third parties, except as described in section 4 and as required by law.
  • It is not used for purposes unrelated to the app's stated functionality.

6. How we protect your data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers travels over HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, never in plain text.
  • Session refresh tokens, password reset codes and email verification codes are stored as SHA-256 hashes.
  • Sessions use short-lived JWT access tokens (15 minutes) with longer-lived refresh tokens that can be revoked.
  • Sign-in and Google sign-in endpoints are rate-limited, and Google ID tokens are verified on our server before any account is opened.
  • Access to the production database and storage is protected by secret keys, and only the roles that need a piece of data can read it.

7. How long we keep your data, and how to delete it

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active, and afterwards only for as long as needed to meet legal obligations or to resolve disputes.

You can delete your account and your data at any time: see our account deletion page or simply email ademseddikadem@gmail.com with your registered email address. Once we receive a valid request, your account and associated personal data are permanently deleted within 30 days, and we confirm the deletion by email.

Data already shared with a shop or a delivery person for a specific completed order may remain with them; we cannot recall a message or an order slip that was already delivered.

8. Your rights

Under Tunisian law, and in particular Loi organique n° 2004-63 on the protection of personal data, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or out of date;
  • Delete your data (see section 7);
  • Object to the use of your data and withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email ademseddikadem@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify that you are the owner of the account.

9. Children

Habichou is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our hosting and service providers — including Supabase, Render, Expo, Mailjet and Google — may store or process data outside Tunisia, including in the European Union and the United States. These providers act on our instructions and are bound to protect your data. By using Habichou, you accept that your data may be transferred to and processed in these countries under the safeguards each provider applies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or in the law. When we do, we revise the effective date shown at the top of this page. Continued use of Habichou after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Effective date: November 26, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?

Open the order from the app. The Steps tab shows the progress of your order, the Map tab shows the courier live on the map, and the Chat tab lets you message the courier directly.

How do I customize my order?

On the supplements screen, choose the sauces and toppings you want. Tap an item up to 3 times to set how much of it you would like — each tap adds one portion.

My order is late or something is wrong.

Open your order and use the Chat tab to talk to the courier first — that is usually the fastest way to sort things out. If that does not help, email us at ademseddikadem@gmail.com with your order details and we will look into it.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Settings in your profile in the app and pick your language: English, French or Tunisian Arabic.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot password and follow the steps — we will email you a code to set a new password.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at ademseddikadem@gmail.com from your registered address and we will delete your account and data. See our account deletion page for what is removed and the timeframe.
